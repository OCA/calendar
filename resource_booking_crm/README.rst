This module links resource bookings to CRM. Each booking type can create
a lead or an opportunity as a follow-up when one of its bookings is
scheduled, linked both ways to the booking and to its calendar meeting.

Bookings also record the UTM campaign, source and medium of the visitor
who made them, and pass them on to the lead. When bookings are made from
a public page (such as the one from ``website_appointment_booking``),
the visitor's UTM cookies are captured automatically, so the lead can be
attributed to the marketing effort that brought the visitor in.

Configuration
=============

Go to *Resource Bookings > Types*, open a booking type and fill in the
**CRM** section:

- **Create in CRM**: *Lead* or *Opportunity*. Leave it empty to create
  nothing for this type.
- **Sales Team**, **Salesperson** and **CRM Tags**: applied to the
  created leads. Without a salesperson, the booking organizer is used,
  or else the first user among the assigned resources.
- **Reuse Open Lead**: link new bookings to the most recent open (not
  won, not archived) lead or opportunity of the requester's company
  instead of creating a new one.

Usage
=====

1. Schedule a booking of a type configured to create CRM leads, from the
   backend, from the portal or from a public booking page.
2. As soon as the booking has a meeting, a lead or opportunity is
   created for the requester, or an open one is reused. A **Lead** smart
   button on the booking opens it, and a **Bookings** smart button on
   the lead lists the bookings linked to it. The meeting also appears on
   the lead.
3. Rescheduling, unscheduling or cancelling the booking keeps the lead.

UTM parameters are stored in cookies when a visitor opens any page of
the website with ``utm_campaign``, ``utm_source`` or ``utm_medium`` in
the URL, and are kept for 31 days. They are captured when the booking is
created by a visitor, never by a salesperson. If the website shows a
cookie bar and the visitor does not accept optional cookies, Odoo does
not store them, so the booking and its lead carry no UTM values.

Go to *Resource Bookings > Types*, open a booking type and fill in the
**CRM** section:

- **Create in CRM**: *Lead* or *Opportunity*. Leave it empty to create
  nothing for this type.
- **Sales Team**, **Salesperson** and **CRM Tags**: applied to the created
  leads. Without a salesperson, the booking organizer is used, or else the
  first user among the assigned resources.
- **Reuse Open Lead**: link new bookings to the most recent open (not won,
  not archived) lead or opportunity of the requester's company instead of
  creating a new one.

This module links resource bookings to CRM. Each booking type can create a
lead or an opportunity as a follow-up when one of its bookings is scheduled,
linked both ways to the booking and to its calendar meeting.

Bookings also record the UTM campaign, source and medium of the visitor who
made them, and pass them on to the lead. When bookings are made from a public
page (such as the one from `website_appointment_booking`), the visitor's UTM
cookies are captured automatically, so the lead can be attributed to the
marketing effort that brought the visitor in.

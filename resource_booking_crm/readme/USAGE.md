1. Schedule a booking of a type configured to create CRM leads, from the
   backend, from the portal or from a public booking page.
2. As soon as the booking has a meeting, a lead or opportunity is created
   for the requester, or an open one is reused. A **Lead** smart button on the
   booking opens it, and a **Bookings** smart button on the lead lists the
   bookings linked to it. The meeting also appears on the lead.
3. Rescheduling, unscheduling or cancelling the booking keeps the lead.

UTM parameters are stored in cookies when a visitor opens any page of the
website with `utm_campaign`, `utm_source` or `utm_medium` in the URL, and are
kept for 31 days. They are captured when the booking is created by a visitor,
never by a salesperson. If the website shows a cookie bar and the visitor does
not accept optional cookies, Odoo does not store them, so the booking and its
lead carry no UTM values.

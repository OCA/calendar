This module glues ``resource_booking`` with ``google_calendar``.

When the organizer of a booking synchronizes their calendar with Google,
Odoo pushes the meeting to Google Calendar and Google invites every
attendee by email. Odoo also sends its own invitation, reschedule and
cancellation emails unless the organizer's Google *access token* is
still valid at that moment. That token expires one hour after the last
synchronization and is only refreshed right before Odoo talks to Google,
which happens after the emails have been sent. In practice the customer
receives two invitations whenever the resource has not opened their
calendar during the last hour.

With this module, meetings created from a resource booking never trigger
the Odoo emails when the organizer has an active Google Calendar
connection: the attendees only receive the invitations sent by Google.
Bookings whose organizer does not synchronize with Google keep receiving
the Odoo emails. Plain meetings without a booking are not affected.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure. The module installs itself as soon as both
``resource_booking`` and ``google_calendar`` are installed.

Each human resource that should let Google send the invitations connects
their Google account from **Calendar > Sync with Google**. The
connection is considered active while the user keeps a refresh token,
has not stopped the synchronization and the synchronization is not
paused globally.

Usage
=====

1. The user who will organize the meetings connects their Google
   Calendar. The organizer is the booking's **Organizer** field: by
   default the user who creates the booking in the backend, or the first
   human resource of the combination for bookings made from a public
   booking page.
2. A booking assigned to that resource is scheduled, from the backend,
   the portal or a public booking page.
3. The meeting is pushed to Google Calendar, which emails the invitation
   to the customer and to the resources. Odoo does not send its own
   invitation, nor the reschedule and cancellation emails for that
   meeting.

If the push to Google fails, the meeting stays flagged for
synchronization and is sent the next time the organizer opens their
calendar or when the Google Calendar synchronization cron runs.
